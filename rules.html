How to Play Crazy 8s

Back to Game

Crazy 8s (also known as Crazy Eights) is a classic shedding-type card game for 2–7 players. Here's everything you need to know to start playing!

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all the cards in your hand.

🃏 Setup

  • Each player is dealt 7 cards (5 cards for 3+ players).
  • The remaining cards form the Draw Pile.
  • The top card is flipped to start the Discard Pile.

🔄 Your Turn

On your turn, play a card from your hand that matches the top card of the Discard Pile by either:

  • Suit (e.g., Hearts on Hearts)
  • Rank (e.g., 6 on 6)

Or play an 8 (Wild Card) at any time.

⭐ Special Cards in Crazy 8s

  • 8 (Wild): Can be played on any card. The player who plays it chooses the suit for the next play.
  • 2 (Draw Two): The next player must draw 2 cards and loses their turn.
  • 7 (Skip): Skips the next player's turn.
  • J (Reverse): Reverses the direction of play (clockwise ↔ counter-clockwise).

📥 Drawing Cards

If you cannot make a valid move (or choose not to), you must draw a card from the Draw Pile.

If the drawn card is playable, you can play it immediately. If not, continue drawing until you find a playable card.

🏆 Winning the Game

The game ends the moment a player has 0 cards left in their hand. That player wins!

💡 Tips & Strategy

  • Save your 8s (Wild Cards) for when you truly need them.
  • Try to keep cards of multiple suits so you always have a playable option.
  • Use Draw Two (2) and Skip (7) cards strategically to slow down opponents.
  • Pay attention to what suits your opponents are playing to block them.
